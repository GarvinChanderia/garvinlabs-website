# **Case Study: FabuLingua (5 New Clients) | In 48 Days With GarvinLabs & myoProcess**

---

#### **Client Overview**

Fabulingua is an innovative educational platform focused on teaching languages to kids through storytelling and interactive learning. They wanted to accelerate their client acquisition by connecting directly with their ideal customers.

#### **Challenge**

Fabulingua faced difficulties scaling their outreach and consistently connecting with the right decision-makers and educators interested in their platform. Their existing inbound methods were slow and unpredictable in delivering qualified leads.

#### **Solution**

GarvinLabs and myoProcess partnered with Fabulingua to design and implement a targeted outbound sales system aimed at their Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). The approach included:

* **Defining the ICP:** Identifying key personas such as schools, language learning centers, and parents actively seeking engaging educational tools.

* **Writing Personalized Outreach That Feels 1:1** Developing tailored messaging and multi-step email campaigns to initiate meaningful conversations.

* **Sales Automation:** Utilizing automation to manage lead capture, follow-ups, and pipeline tracking efficiently.

* **Lead Nurturing:** Implementing processes to qualify and nurture prospects, guiding them smoothly toward conversion.

#### **Results 🚀**

Within 48 days of high intensity campaign sending, Fabulingua achieved:

* **5 New Clients Acquired:** Directly resulting from targeted outreach and effective engagement.

* **$8,288 Initial Investment:** The setup and campaign execution delivered a strong return on investment.

* **Faster Pipeline Movement:** Qualified leads progressed swiftly, reducing sales cycle time.

* **A Scalable Sales System:** Fabulingua now has a repeatable, automated outbound process to fuel ongoing growth.

### **Case Study: Hippocratic AI (2 New Partners) | In 60 Days With GarvinLabs & myoProcess**

#### **Client Overview**

Hippocratic AI is a healthcare technology company creating AI solutions designed to help hospitals and healthcare providers make better decisions and improve patient care. They wanted to grow faster by connecting with the right people who truly needed their technology.

#### **Challenge**

Even with great tech, Hippocratic AI found it hard to stand out in a busy healthcare market. Their usual marketing and inbound efforts were slow and didn’t bring in enough qualified leads. They needed a simpler, more reliable way to reach hospital decision-makers and build meaningful partnerships.

#### **Solution**

That’s where we stepped in, together with myoProcess. We worked closely with Hippocratic AI to create a focused outbound sales system designed to connect with exactly the right people. Here’s what we did:

* **Getting Clear on the Ideal Customers:** We helped pinpoint hospital admins, tech leaders, and healthcare innovators who would benefit most from their AI solutions.

* **Crafting Personalized, Genuine Outreach:** Instead of generic emails, we created messages that felt thoughtful and 1:1, making it easier to start real conversations.

* **Setting Up Smart Automation:** We put in place tools that kept track of leads, sent timely follow-ups, and kept everything running smoothly.

* **Nurturing Relationships:** We guided prospects along the journey so that interested leads didn’t fall through the cracks.

#### **Results 🚀**

In just 60 days, Hippocratic AI landed 2 new partnership deals worth $35,000, all from connecting with the right people at the right time. Their sales process became quicker, and the whole team felt more confident in their outreach approach.

## **How we did it? 🚀**

**The idea is simple, yet most don't implement it:**

**Targeting prospects based on their specific pain points**, not just their job titles or industries.

Here’s how we approached it:

### **1\. Identifying Client Pain Points**

We worked with Crawford Thomas to uncover the **core frustrations** of their target clients, such as:

* **Labor shortages**: Manufacturing companies struggling to find skilled workers.  
* **Inefficient hiring processes**: Clients frustrated by long hiring cycles and unqualified candidates.  
* **Retention issues**: Companies losing employees due to poor job matches.

By understanding these pain points, we tailored messaging that resonated deeply with their prospects.

---

### **2\. Systematic Outreach & Scalability**

We implemented an **automated email outreach system** that sent personalized messages at scale. Instead of generic cold emails, we focused on:

* **Addressing specific frustrations** prospects were facing.  
* Using **calibrated questions** to spark meaningful conversations.  
* Highlighting how Crawford Thomas could solve their hiring challenges.

---

### **3\. Managing Lead Overflow**

Our strategy generated a **significant increase in leads**, so we helped Crawford Thomas:

* **Prioritize high-quality leads**, ensuring their team focused on the most promising opportunities.  
* **Segment leads** based on urgency and potential, streamlining their workflow.

---

### **4\. Ensuring Lead Quality**

We maintained a high standard for lead quality by:

* Continuously optimizing the outreach process.  
* Regularly reviewing responses to refine messaging and improve targeting.

---

## **The Advertising Cost Spiral 🏹**

Before working with us, Crawford Thomas was stuck in a common trap: relying on costly PPC campaigns to generate leads. These campaigns often resulted in high CPAs (cost per acquisition) without addressing the root problem—**ineffective prospecting**.

By shifting to a **targeted, pain-point-driven approach**, we helped them break free from this cycle, saving money and improving results.

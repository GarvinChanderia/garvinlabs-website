# **Case Study: How We Routed 4 Qualified Introductions for Lasting Blueprint Productions in 78 Days**

Most video production companies make the same mistake when they try to get clients: they sell “video.”

**That sounds obvious, but it is actually the problem.**

“Do you need a video?” is too broad. Everyone technically needs video, (duh)

Nobody wakes up thinking, “I need video production.” They wake up thinking: “We have a conference coming up.” “We need to document this training.” “We need better donor material.” “We need customer proof.” “We need to show stakeholders what happened.” “We need polished content from this event.” “We need a video our team can use after the room is empty.”

That distinction is where the route opened.

Lasting Blueprint Productions is an Orlando / Central Florida video production company. On the surface, they can help with a lot: event video, corporate video, training videos, testimonials, promotional content, and editing. But “anyone who needs video” is not a market. It is a fog. The real ICP was much sharper: organizations in Orlando and Central Florida that already have real-world business activity happening and need that activity documented professionally.

That means conferences, corporate events, trade shows, conventions, internal training programs, stakeholder presentations, nonprofit programs, healthcare communication, testimonials, onboarding, compliance, and customer-facing proof.

The strongest route was not tiny local businesses asking for one random promo video. It was event-driven and operations-heavy organizations that already had something worth capturing.

That changed the whole target.

We were not looking for “video buyers.” We were looking for organizations with moments, programs, rooms, people, and proof already moving in the real world.

The best-fit buckets became clear fast.

Corporate event teams and conference organizers were an obvious lane because Orlando is one of the strongest event and convention markets in the country. If a company is hosting a summit, trade show, launch, panel, internal event, or multi-room program, the video need is not abstract. The room is happening. The speakers are there. The audience is there. The content either gets captured properly or disappears.

Nonprofits and associations were another strong fit because they often need event coverage, donor-facing videos, community program documentation, training content, and stakeholder presentations. For these buyers, video is not just marketing. It is memory, proof, fundraising material, and internal communication.

Healthcare, professional services, and local business groups also made sense because trust is the product. Dentists, clinics, law firms, and service businesses need intro videos, testimonials, patient or client stories, and proof assets that make them feel real before someone books.

Construction, field-service, and training-heavy companies were another route because their video needs are operational. Procedures, safety videos, demonstrations, onboarding modules, compliance training, internal processes. These are not vanity projects. They are business infrastructure.

And because Lasting Blueprint Productions is MBE and DBE certified, there was also a public-sector-adjacent angle: agencies, universities, municipalities, and larger contractors that need certified diverse vendors for video and event production work.

That was the real repositioning.

Not:

“We help with video production.”

But:

“We help Orlando and Central Florida organizations professionally document the business activity they already have happening — events, training, testimonials, internal communication, and proof projects.”

Once the market was framed that way, outreach became much easier because we were not trying to create demand from nothing. We were looking for buyers with visible triggers: upcoming events, annual conferences, training programs, nonprofit campaigns, stakeholder meetings, public programs, customer proof needs, and organizations whose work would become more valuable if it was captured properly.

Over 78 days, we directly routed 4 qualified introductions for Lasting Blueprint Productions.

The important part is not just the number. The important part is why those introductions were possible.

They came from narrowing the market from a generic service category into a real buying situation.

A video company does not need to chase everyone with a camera-shaped pitch. It needs to understand where video becomes urgent, useful, and tied to a real business event.

That is the whole lesson.

If someone runs a conference, they need the room captured.

If a nonprofit runs donor or community programs, they need proof.

If a company trains staff, they need repeatable assets.

If a healthcare or professional services firm sells trust, they need testimonials and human proof.

If a construction or field-service company has procedures, compliance, or safety needs, they need internal video that makes the work easier to teach.

Same service. Different market frame.

And that frame is what creates the route.

For Lasting Blueprint Productions, the ICP was never “anyone who needs video.” The ICP was Orlando / Central Florida organizations with real activity already happening that needed to be documented, packaged, and reused.

That is how we went from broad video production positioning to 4 direct routed introductions in 78 days.
